Dictionary of German Words:
• German = English
• Ohrwurm = Earworm (such as a catchy song)
• Pantoffelheld = Henpecked Husband (person controlled by their partner - technical translation is slipper hero)
• Sauerkraut = Fermented Cabage (technical translation is sour cabbage)
• Hauptstrasse = Main Street
• Broetchen = Bread Roll
• Klimaanlage = Air Conditioning
• Rammstein = German Metal Band
• Porsche = German Car Brand 
• hundemuede = Dog-Tired (extremely tired)
• Biergarten = Beer Garden
• Drahtesel = Bicycle (technical translation is wire donkey)
• Dirndel = Traditional Dress
• Staubsauger = Vacuum Cleaner (technical translation is dust sucker)
• wettbewerbsfaehig = Competitive
• Mehlhase = Flour Hare
• Gebaeck = Baked Good
• Baeckerei = Bakery
• Hund = Dog
• Doener = Kebab
• Apfelkuchen = Apple Cake
• Handschuhe = Gloves (technical translation is hand shoes)
